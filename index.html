09 — Questions, answered

Good to
know.

Have another question? Get in touch directly →

Are courses available online? +

Yes. All our courses are available via live online sessions with interactive Q&A, as well as offline classroom options across South India. Contact us for current batch schedules.

Who can join the Gemology course? +

Our programs are designed for beginners, gemstone enthusiasts, jewellery business owners, astrology practitioners, and students looking for a professional career in gemology.

Do you offer Advanced Gemology certification? +

Yes. The Advanced Gemology program covers synthetic separation, heat treatment identification, spectroscopy, and commercial gemstone testing equipment.

Are natural certified gemstones available? +

Yes. We provide 100% natural, unheated, and untreated certified gemstones for retail and wholesale enquiries with official laboratory test reports.

Can I request custom astrological jewellery? +

Yes. We design custom 22K gold and pure silver rings, pendants, and bracelets with open-back settings so the gemstone touches the skin naturally.
